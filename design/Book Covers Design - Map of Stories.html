✦ The design schema制式

A colour illustration of the book's setting — sky, land and weather rendered in a flat, celestial palette — wrapped in the atlas's signature gold double-keyline with corner ticks. A deep-navy scrim grades up from the foot so the title always reads, and the setting's real coordinates are engraved at the bottom. Vivid scene, constant frame: the covers look like one series even as each place glows its own colour.

The reusable prompt

Design a book cover in the "Map of Stories" illustrated-atlas style: a 2:3 portrait. Fill the whole cover with a flat, layered colour illustration of the book's setting at its most evocative hour and weather — a gradient sky, silhouetted land or skyline, and fine atmospheric particles (rain, snow, stars, light). Commit to one signature palette drawn from the place — [e.g. crimson + gold for Istanbul; cold slate-blue + one amber window for St Petersburg] — and scatter a few faint gold stars to echo the atlas. Then wrap it in the fixed atlas frame, identical on every cover: • a gold double keyline inset from the edge with right-angle ticks at the corners • a deep-navy (#0b1730) scrim grading up from the bottom ~55% so text stays legible • a faint spine shadow down the left edge Typeset over the scrim, bottom-anchored: • genre eyebrow in tracked gold capitals — "A NOVEL" • title in Cormorant Garamond 600 (#f6ecd2), balanced on two lines — [TITLE] • a 30px gold rule, then the author in EB Garamond italic — [AUTHOR] • a foot line: ✦ + the setting's real coordinates in gold capitals — [LAT° · LNG°] Vivid painted scene, constant gold frame. Gold on navy is the binding; the picture inside changes per book.
